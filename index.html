— About me

Engineering
since I was 16.

I'm a software engineer based in Matagalpa, Nicaragua, with deep experience shipping enterprise software end-to-end — from architecting cloud-native services to modernizing decade-old legacy stacks.

Today I split my time between two things I love: building reliable money-movement software at Remitly in Go and React Native, and growing Vanter, the SaaS company I founded in March 2026 to design high-performance products for sectors that are usually stuck on spreadsheets.

I've led teams through Java 8 → 21 migrations, replaced raw JDBC with session-per-request Hibernate, hardened Spring Security boundaries, and delivered 100% production uptime on systems that used to crash daily. I care about correctness, clarity and maintainability — in that order.
